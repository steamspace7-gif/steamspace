Field Trips with a Purpose

At the Steamspace students build hands-on projects that are tied to their grade level AZ standards while being introduced to a variety of new experiences and possible lifelong interests. We offer projects including laser cutting and engraving and design, 3D design and printing, electronic circuits, micro:bit and vex robotics programming, as well as other building and making opportunities all located at Building 106 on the Ft. Apache Historic Park.
