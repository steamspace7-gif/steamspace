3rd Grade

Intro to Paper Circuits

Students follow the FIGUROUT engineering process to design and build light-up greeting cards.

Students design and build light-up greeting cards using copper tape, LEDs, and coin-cell batteries — learning circuits through a creative, take-home project.

Schedule This Field Trip

Field Trip Preparation

Before the visit, explore interests and careers, discuss what students would like to make, and watch this short project overview: YouTube preview.

F — Find Out About Circuits

Learn about circuits and complete the Electric Light and Switch Snap Circuit activity. Media: project overview video.

I — Identify the Challenge

Look at example cards that include LED lights. What kind of card would you like? Who will you give it to?

G — Guide Yourself With a Goal

Plan a first-draft prototype. A prototype is a practice copy of your card. On folded paper: write your message, draw what should light up, and decide where the switch and light will go.

U — Use Your Imagination

Make the card personal. Who will receive it? What will you say? What images would they enjoy?

R — Review Your Prototype Carefully

Before building the final card, confirm you have: the words you want to say, the picture you want to draw, where the LED will go, and where the touch switch will go. Show your plan to a teacher before moving on.

O — OK, Start Building

Choose heavier cardstock for the final version. Draw the image, write the words, then tape the light and switch into place.

U — Understand There Will Be Difficulties

If the card does not light up: check that the long LED leg connects to the + side, make sure the maker tape on the switch touches when pressed, look for breaks in the tape, test the LED directly on the battery, and ask for help.

T — Take Time to Evaluate Your Project

Congratulate yourself for a job well done — then share what worked and what you would improve next time.
