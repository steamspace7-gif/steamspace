Students follow the FIGUROUT engineering process to design and build light-up greeting cards using copper tape, LEDs, and coin-cell batteries.

U - FIGUROUT - Understand There Will be Difficulties

Select section U - FIGUROUT - Understand There Will be Difficulties Collapse Expand U - FIGUROUT - Understand There Will be Difficulties Remember if your card does not light up do these things: Check to make sure the long leg of your LED is connected to the + side Make sure your the maker tape on your switch is touching when you push it. Make sure there are no breaks in your tape between the battery and the LED Test the LED to see if it works when you connect it directly to the battery. ASK for HELP!
