Intro to Paper Circuits

3rd Grade

Students use the FIGUROUT engineering/design process to prototype and build personalized interactive greeting cards with copper tape, LEDs, and coin cell batteries.

Schedule This Field Trip

What Students Do

Watch introductory videos and Snap Circuits demos, identify the challenge of creating a light-up card, measure and cut copper tape, place and test the LED with polarity awareness, create a touch switch, assemble a closed circuit on cardstock with a coin cell battery, troubleshoot common issues, and decorate for personalization.

3rd Grade Arizona Standards

Math: 3.MD.B.4 (measurement with halves/fourths), 3.G.A.2 (partitioning shapes). Computer Science: 3.CS.HS.01 (hardware troubleshooting), 3.CS.NI.01 (model electricity flow). Science: 3.P4U1.3 (energy transfer). Plus ISTE-inspired Standard 1.1 (basic technology operation).

Materials

Cardstock, copper tape, 5mm LEDs, CR2032 coin cell batteries, maker tape or foil for touch switches, rulers, scissors, markers, clear tape, and optional templates.

Time

60–90 minutes, or two sessions for planning/prototyping and building/testing.
