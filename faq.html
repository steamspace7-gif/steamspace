Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers for planning your class visit.

We currently offer hands-on projects designed for 2nd–6th grade students. Current projects by grade: 2nd LEGO Paper Building, 3rd Paper Circuits, 4th Lasercut, 5th Micro:bit, and 6th Puzzle. Each project is tied to Arizona grade-level standards.

Most trips run 2–3 hours, not including travel and lunch. We can adjust the schedule based on your bus availability and whether you want to visit the Apache Cultural Center and Museum.

Field trips are offered on Tuesdays and Thursdays during the school year. Check our calendar on the Schedule page for open dates.

Students should bring a signed permission slip, a sack lunch if staying through lunch, and weather-appropriate clothing. We provide all project materials and tools.

Projects are designed for groups of 20 students or fewer. Larger classes can be split into rotating groups.

Yes. Schools are responsible for providing permission slips, transportation, and adequate adult supervision. We recommend at least one adult per 10 students.

Choose a project on the Choose page, then visit the Schedule page to check availability and fill out the Field Trip Interest Form. We will follow up to confirm details.

Visit our Donate page for ways to contribute supplies and support Steamspace.

We are located at Building 106 on the Fort Apache Historic Park. A map is available on the Confirm page.
