Lasercut Boxes

4th Grade

Students design and laser-cut custom boxes using vector graphics, learning about measurement, kerf, tabs, and geometric design.

Schedule This Field Trip

What Students Do

Explore laser engraving and cutting, learn vector graphics and kerf, design a box template in software, export an SVG file, and watch the laser machine cut their box. Optional extensions include hinges, lids, and decorative engravings.

4th Grade Arizona Standards

Math: 4.MD.A.1 (measurement units and conversions), 4.MD.C.5 (angles), 4.G.A.1 (lines and shapes), 4.MD.A.3 (area/perimeter). Science: 4.P4U1.1 (energy transfer), 4.E1U2.10 (engineering design), SEP practices. Visual Arts: VA.CR.1.4, VA.CR.2.4, VA.CR.3.4, VA.PR.5.4. Computer Science: 4.CS.D.01 (digital tools), 4.AP.PD.05 (debugging), 4.AP.A.01 (algorithms).

Materials

Draft board or tempered hardboard (1/8"), acrylic markers, computer access with Inkscape or box template software, laser cutter/engraver.

Time

One full field trip session, including introduction, digital design, laser cutting, and assembly.

Full lesson plans, worksheets, and media are available in the Steamspace Moodle course.
