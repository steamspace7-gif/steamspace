What Is Laser Engraving?

Laser engraving machines produce a tiny laser beam that superheats a very small area of the surface being engraved. The beam melts or vaporizes the top layers to leave a permanent mark. Because only the areas touched by the laser are heated, precise images can be engraved into almost any hard surface without becoming blurry.

Field Trip Preparation

Explore interests and careers, discuss design goals, and practice designing a box template before the visit.

Creating a Box Template on the Computer

Use Makercase or Boxes.py to make a box template online. Design your box top with a border first, then add SVG shapes (lines only) and personalized text. SVG files for shapes are available in the Moodle course folder.

Laser Machine Steps

Cutting settings: Select cut and engrave lines, choose correct speed and power from material charts, and run the cut preview in LightBurn.

Material setup: Position material securely, set focal distance if needed, and frame the design on the laser bed so material sits under the framed area.

Begin burning: Turn on the laser, start the process on the computer, and monitor the machine while it cuts.
