Laser Cut Boxes

Select section Laser Cut Boxes Collapse Expand Laser Cut Boxes Collapse all Expand all Select activity Text and media area WHAT IS LASER ENGRAVING? Laser engraving machines produce a tiny laser beam that superheats a very small area of the surface being engraved. The beam melts or vaporizes the top layers of the surface to leave a permanent mark. The color of the laser engravings or marks left behind depends on the material. Because laser engraving only heats the areas touched by the laser, very precise images can be engraved into almost any hard surface without the image becoming blurry or losing focus.
