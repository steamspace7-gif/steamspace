Micro:bit Reaction Game

5th Grade

Students build a physical game board with touch-sensitive foil switches and program a Micro:bit to create a two-player reaction-time challenge.

Schedule This Field Trip

What Students Do

Build a physical game board from cardboard and foil to create touch-sensitive switches, program a Micro:bit in MakeCode with events, loops, conditionals, and randomness, then test reaction times and iterate on their design.

5th Grade Arizona Standards

Computer Science: 3-5.CS.02 (debug), 3-5.AP.10 (algorithms), 3-5.AP.13 (decompose problems), 3-5.AP.14 (sequences/events/loops/conditionals), 3-5.IC.19 (responsible tech use). Science: 5.P3U1.4, 3-5.SEP.1, 3-5.SEP.3, 3-5.SEP.4, 3-5.SEP.6, 3-5.CC.2. Math: 5.MD.B.2 (represent/interpret data).

Materials

BBC Micro:bit, battery holder with 2 AAA batteries, crocodile clip leads, cardboard, aluminum foil, glue, scissors, markers, computer/tablet with MakeCode (makecode.microbit.org), USB cable.

Time

90–120 minutes, or two sessions: one for building the board and one for coding and testing.
