Collapse Expand AZ 5th Grade Standards Select activity AZ Standards Overview The Micro:bit reaction game lesson involves students building a simple physical game board using cardboard and tin foil to create touch-sensitive switches/electrodes, then programming a Micro:bit (likely via MakeCode blocks or similar) to create a two-player reaction time challenge. The Micro:bit typically waits a random delay, shows a signal (like a heart or arrow), and measures who touches/completes their circuit first by pressing foil connected to pins (e.g., GND and P0/P1), displaying results or declaring a winner.
