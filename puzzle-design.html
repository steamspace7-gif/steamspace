Puzzle Design

6th Grade

Students design a custom 2D jigsaw puzzle in Inkscape inspired by cultural and modern geometric art, then laser-cut their puzzles while applying 6th grade math.

Schedule This Field Trip

What Students Do

Create a vector-based puzzle design in Inkscape, incorporate artistic elements inspired by Navajo rugs, pottery, pictographs, and modern patterns, scale designs to a precise 15 cm × 20 cm template, use the Lasercut Jigsaw extension to generate puzzle pieces, save as SVG, and observe/produce laser-cut versions. Optional coloring with acrylic paint pens.

6th Grade Arizona Standards

Math: 6.G.A.1 (area of polygons), 6.G.A.3 (coordinate plane polygons), 6.RP.A.3 (ratio/rate/scaling). Science & Engineering: develop and use models, use mathematics and computational thinking, construct explanations and design solutions, plan and carry out investigations. Engineering design process embedded throughout.

Materials

Puzzle Design Worksheet (PDF), computers with Inkscape and the Lasercut Jigsaw extension, projector/shared screen, art inspiration slideshow, 15 cm × 20 cm template reference, acrylic paint pens (optional), student sketches, laser cutter/engraver.

Time

4–5 hours for a full single-day field trip, including arrival/safety, math worksheet, design in Inkscape, laser cutting, and reflection.

Full lesson plans, worksheets, and media are available in the Steamspace Moodle course.
