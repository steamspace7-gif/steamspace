Steamspace Interest Inventory

If your class has time before the trip, this optional warm-up can spark curiosity and help students spot strengths and interests—including hobbies and paths they might enjoy. Results are for learning and conversation, not grades.

Students answer simple “how much like me?” questions across eight areas: Build & Make, Explore & Figure Out, Create & Express, Help & Care, Lead & Speak Up, Organize & Plan, Nature & Outdoors, and Move & Play. Each form asks for name, grade, school, and teacher at the top.
